Our story

Small shop.
Big blooms.

Magic Flowers started with one idea: Bhopal deserves a florist that treats every order — a ₹300 rose bunch or a full wedding stage — with the same attention to detail. We source fresh daily, wrap by hand, and keep our doors open long after most shops have closed, because celebrations don't run on office hours.

  • Fresh flowers sourced and arranged fresh every single day
  • Full wedding and birthday decor, from mandaps to balloon arches
  • Bonsai, potted plants, handmade and artificial flowers in stock
  • Customised bouffes pairing flowers with chocolates and gifts
  • Same-day delivery across Bhopal, orders taken on WhatsApp
